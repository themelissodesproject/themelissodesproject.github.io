Nesting Habits and Parasites of Certain Bees of Boulder County, Colorado

Melissodes menuachus, Natural Enemies & Biotic Interactions, Life History & Nesting Biology, Behavior, Melissodes menuachus nesting behavior, Melissodes mixeae nesting behavior, Melissodes menuachus cleptoparasitism by Triepeolus occidentalis, Melissodes mixeae cleptoparasitism by Triepeolus occidentalis, Triepeolus occidentalis cleptoparasite of Melissodes menuachus, Melissodes menuachus ground nest provisioning, Melissodes menuachus pollen collection, Melissodes menuachus Boulder County Colorado, Melissodes mixeae Boulder County Colorado, Triepeolus occidentalis, cleptoparasite
Reports timed field observations of a Melissodes menuachus (as M. mixeae in the paper) ground nest near Boulder, Colorado, tracking pollen-provisioning visits, alongside two separate instances in which a female Triepeolus occidentalis Cresson (a cleptoparasitic bee) entered the nest entrance while the host bee was provisioning. The author considers this evidence for a parasite-host relationship between the two species.
