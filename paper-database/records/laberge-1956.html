This is Part I of LaBerge's monographic revision of Melissodes for North and Central America, so far covering the introduction, phylogenetic and geographic-variation discussion, the key to subgenera, the complete subgenus Brachymelissodes (cressonii, minima), and a large part of subgenus Epimelissodes: full species accounts for M. atripes, M. albocollaris, M. grandissima, the newly described M. aegis, M. comanche, M. machaerantherae, M. helianthelli, the newly described M. nitida, an extensive account of M. obliqua with three subspecies (obliqua, expurgata, caliginosa) including a nesting-biology account (Custer, Colorado) describing Triepeolus concavus as a cleptoparasite, M. texana with the new subspecies M. texana eluta, M. petulca with its two subspecies (petulca, suffusa; with illinoensis and townsendi treated as synonyms), including a geographic pie-graph analysis of the Texas intergradation zone between the subspecies and extensive flower records dominated by Compositae (especially Helianthus (sunflower), Ratibida, Gaillardia, Rudbeckia, Coreopsis, and Helenium), and the start of the species account of M. sabinensis (Epimelissodes), a species of three largely allopatric subspecies distinguished from petulca suffusa mainly by male mesepisternal hair density and genitalic characters.
