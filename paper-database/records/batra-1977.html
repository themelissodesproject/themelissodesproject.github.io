Nests of Centris, Melissodes, and Colletes in Guatemala (Hymenoptera: Apoidea)

Batra, S. W. T., Schuster, J. C. (1977). Biotropica.

This paper gives one of the few available nest descriptions for the genus Melissodes, documenting a complete nest of Melissodes floris (subgenus Eumelissodes) found within a dense multi-species bee aggregation on a roadside bank near San Miguel Duenas, Guatemala. It reports burrow and cell dimensions, provision character, and notes that three dead M. floris females each carried a puparium of a phorid fly (Phoridae) in the thorax, indicating parasitism of the bee by this fly.
An uncommon interspecific dense aggregation of nests constructed by the solitary bees Centris transversa, C. anthracina, Melissodes floris, Colletes sp., and Anthophora sp. occupied a steep bank in Guatemala. The nests, cells, provisions, and brood of the first four species are described and illustrated for the first time. A Centris inquiline was Coelioxys sp. (Megachilidae); Melissodes females were parasitized by phorid flies.
