How Efficient Are Bees in Pollinating Sunflowers?

Parker, F. D. (1981). Journal of the Kansas Entomological Society.

Melissodes agilis, Pollination Biology, Behavior (General), General Ecology, Melissodes agilis pollination efficiency, Melissodes agilis sunflower pollinator, Melissodes pollen load per visit, Melissodes vs honey bee seed set, Melissodes male patrolling and clustering behavior, Melissodes floral fidelity sunflower rows, Melissodes oligolecty on sunflower, Melissodes as most abundant wild bee
This field study at Logan, Utah compares pollination efficiency of native bees versus honey bees on sunflower, finding Melissodes agilis (both sexes) to be the most effective and most faithful pollinator across all sterile/fertile line combinations, producing far more seeds per visit than Apis mellifera and carrying substantially more pollen (up to ~104,500 grains per female) than any other bee group studied.
