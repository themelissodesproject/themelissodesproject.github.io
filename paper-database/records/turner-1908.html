THE SUN-DANCE OF MELISSODES.

Turner, C. H. (1908). Psyche.

Behavior (General), Mating & Reproductive Behavior, Parasitism & Parasitoidism, Natural Enemies (General), Melissodes sun-dance courtship display, Melissodes male mating swarm behavior, Melissodes nuptial pursuit and ambuscade, Melissodes female pollen return flight, Melissodes burrow nesting aggregation Augusta Georgia, Nomadidae (cuckoo bees) cleptoparasite of Melissodes, Melissodes clypeus color sexual dimorphism, Melissodes coition behavior, Nomadidae, cleptoparasite
This 1908 field note describes a male mating aggregation display ('sun-dance') observed at a Melissodes nesting site in Augusta, Georgia, in which yellow-clypeus males repeatedly chase, dodge, and grapple with black-clypeus females returning to their burrows carrying pollen loads, which Turner interprets as a 'nuptial ambuscade' facilitating copulation. It also notes Nomadidae (cuckoo bees), a cleptoparasitic bee family, loitering near Melissodes burrows waiting to oviposit on the bees' stored pollen provisions.
